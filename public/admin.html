Policies are enforced for every non-administrator account: a file is listed, previewed, downloaded or changed only when every policy on its path allows it — not locked, published, inside its availability window, and, if restricted, granted to that user. A policy on a folder covers everything inside it. Paths with no policy stay open to all signed-in users, and administrators are never restricted. "Public" currently behaves like "Authenticated": every request needs a sign-in.
