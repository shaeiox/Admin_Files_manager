How the file portal decides access

Policies apply to the file portal only. This app is never restricted by them: every account that signs in here manages the whole storage. On the portal, each policy gives a visitor or signed-in user one of two levels.

  1. ViewListed in browse, search, categories, releases and the navbar, marked "View only"
  2. DownloadAlso downloaded, checksummed, zipped and served as the latest release
  • A policy on a folder caps everything inside it; the lowest level on the path wins.
  • A deny, for the user or one of their groups, beats every grant.
  • Unpublished, or outside its availability dates, hides it from everyone.
  • Paths with no policy stay open to every portal visitor. Administrators get no bypass on the portal: they follow the same policies as everyone else.
